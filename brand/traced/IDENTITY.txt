PRODUCT PROMISE
Traced connects claims to exact sources, shows what an assessment depends on,
and helps reviewers challenge evidence, record human decisions, and share a
checkable evidence dossier. Its product direction brings this process earlier:
helping teams review emerging information risks and prepare evidence-led
prebunking responses as use cases, source coverage and alert methods are
developed and validated.

The shared process is define purpose and scope -> collect or import -> inspect
baselines and signals -> check exact evidence, compare and challenge -> human
review and reversible exclusions -> briefing and evidence dossier.

This describes the product direction. Claims about available collection,
monitoring, alerts, coverage and outcomes require current verification.
The branches represent evidence lineage. The clock arc represents chronology.
The coloured endpoint marks a point of focus; it is not a truth or confidence
rating. Branch Clock is a logo name, not a new product feature or data model.

WORKSHOP IDEAS AND PRESENTATION CLAIMS
The product direction draws on the source-mapped workshop ideas in
docs/source-notes/2026-11-06-workshop-ocr.md. The transcription preserves
uncertain handwriting, proposals and unverified claims separately.

Subscription pricing, proposed customer segments, and EMEA/NA/APAC applicability
are business hypotheses. Proposed platforms and investigation horizons describe
ideas to validate; they do not establish current coverage or an active monitor.
The handwritten alert percentage requires a metric, baseline, window, coverage
and review status before it can become an interpretable candidate rule.

Workshop political, payment and bot-count claims remain unverified source notes.
Use source-grounded demonstrations and label fictional examples explicitly.
Presentation support should explain the review process and its evidence, with
clear differences between implemented, locally checked and proposed behaviour.

A dossier contains traceable saved records, citations, reviewed interpretations,
qualifications and decision history. Platform-policy research needs its own
source and retrieval date. Preparing a dossier, submitting a report and a
platform's decision are separate steps. Product messaging must keep their
readiness and outcomes distinct; it cannot promise a successful takedown.

NAMING ACROSS THE PROJECT
Use Traced in the main product heading, browser title, common app chrome, and
new branded briefing/export covers as their owning chats adopt this identity.
Keep specialist names as useful descriptors: Evidence Explorer, Evidence Trail,
Swarm Detector, and Historical Review and Chronology retain their technical or
specialist meanings. A specialist surface can say, for example, "Swarm Detector"
with "Part of Traced" as a supporting label.

This identity decision does not request renaming Python packages, directories,
CLI commands, schemas, chats, repositories, branches, or existing pull requests.
Preserve open PR numbers, history, and discussion. Do not change evidence origin
labels, run IDs, case IDs, or timestamps to fit a brand convention.

VISUAL SYSTEM
Ink       #142229  Primary text, structure, dark surfaces.
Paper     #F4F3EE  Main reading background.
White     #FFFFFF  Elevated reading panels and cards.
Vermilion #F36643  Brand endpoint and primary action fill.
Slate     #52636C  Supporting text and essential control outlines.
Line      #D9E0DE  Decorative dividers only.
Moss      #276B63  Supporting data and links on light surfaces.

Typography: Manrope for headings, interface and body; IBM Plex Mono for source
identifiers, timestamps and compact metadata. Local fonts and OFL licences are
included under fonts/. Use traced-tokens.css and traced-tokens.json rather than
independently recreating theme values in each feature.

Use Ink text on Vermilion actions (5.26:1 contrast). Slate/Paper and Moss/Paper
both exceed 5.6:1. Do not use small white text on orange. Use a visible Ink focus
ring on light backgrounds and a Paper ring on dark backgrounds. Keep errors,
review-needed states, and completion distinct from the brand accent and label
them in words. Never make green mean that a finding is automatically true.

Keep the logo proportions fixed. Leave clear space of at least the diameter of
the coloured endpoint around the visible artwork. Minimum lockup width: 120 px
or 30 mm. Minimum standalone symbol: 24 px or 8 mm. Use the supplied favicon for
smaller digital uses. Keep the logo away from busy evidence images and graphs.
Use a disciplined grid, generous reading space, restrained 8 px card radii, and
one clear primary action. Preserve the readable focused graphs already built.

VOICE AND EVIDENCE LANGUAGE
Write like a careful colleague: calm, direct, specific and open about limits.
Keep Observation, Inference, Assumption, model proposal, specialist result and
human review decision distinguishable. Preserve Use, Qualify and Withhold.
Say "supported by", "requires review", and "unknown" where appropriate.
Exact citations provide traceability; they do not alone establish truth,
independence, intent, attribution or a historical event time.

ADOPTION AND OWNERSHIP
The branding chat owns this directory. The coordination chat owns the shared
implementation plan. The UI/workflow chat leads visual adoption with the common
review/layout owner and platform owner. Feature chats inherit the common theme
and apply terminology in their own surfaces; they do not create parallel themes
or take over shared files without coordinating ownership.

Each owner keeps its current authorized feature work. Apply the identity at the
next safe integration point. Respect an active shared verification freeze; do
not interrupt live collection/model checks or start competing workers.

A visual adoption handoff names the files changed, visible surfaces checked,
screenshots or artifacts, accessibility checks, remaining gaps and next owner.
Check headings, browser title, app header, favicon, empty/error/loading states,
source inspection, existing graph readability, narrow layout, and print/export
headers where that owner changes them. Preserve exact evidence, decisions,
data-directory selection and export/reopen semantics.

PUBLICATION LIMIT
Publishing this identity and notifying chats is distinct from completing the
visual rollout in every application. The branding chat reports deliveries;
the implementation owners report what they have actually applied and checked.
